Transformation through Mind

Neuroplasticity, also known as neural or brain plasticity, refers to the brain's remarkable ability to reorganize itself by forming new neural connections throughout life. It's the brain's capacity to adapt in response to experiences, learning, and even damage. This adaptive process involves changes in neural pathways, the strength of connections between neurons (synapses), and even the physical structure of the brain.
